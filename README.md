I build AI products that run in production, and I still write the code. Most of my work lives in private repositories for clients and employers, so this page describes it; the public projects are below.

## What I'm building now

**At CyberX** (cybersecurity consultancy, Porto), where I lead development:

- **Private LLM platform.** EU-resident assistant on Azure with self-hosted models (Ollama, Qwen) and RAG over the company knowledge base, so client data never leaves the tenant. I cut its response time from 211 s to 13 s after tracing the delay to hidden reasoning tokens.
- **GuardianX**, pentest management: React, TypeScript, Vite, TanStack Query and Firebase, with TOTP MFA, role-based access, Vitest and Playwright tests, and a deploy on every merge.
- **The O**, an OSINT platform of 21 containerised services: NLP pipelines (multilingual sentiment, spaCy NER, OCR) and a React analyst UI, with an LLM client that falls back between providers.
- **MailX**, email marketing and phishing simulation in English, Portuguese and Arabic (RTL).

**At blue·auth**, my own product: document signing and digital evidence on TypeScript, React and Firebase, with a remote **MCP server** so AI agents can sign and verify documents while a person approves every write.
